Click a cell to select it. Press “.” to toggle a block on/off (clicking never does). Type letters to fill; Backspace clears the current letter and steps back one press at a time, then removes the block behind it on the next press from an already-empty cell. Double-click a cell, press Space, or use arrow keys to change across/down direction. ⌘F / Ctrl+F fills the puzzle; ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undoes.
